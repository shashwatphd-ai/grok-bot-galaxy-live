## What Day 1 established (visual record)

1. **The demo company is an event-venue business**: an event platform (users create events with date/time, sessions, venue, staff), ticket and badge design (night-market stubs, lanyards), a venue with cabin lodging ("Pine Cabins", family bookings) and activities (glider flights), plus merch including Grok Bot plushies. Food appeared in the original idea dialogue and in the venue/catering context the stage discussed.
2. **No public website launched on Day 1.** A staging sign-up page appeared on screen; the URL was never shown legibly, and nothing is publicly indexed.
3. **The bot fleet grew from 11 named characters to 30+** (28 on the 4:19 PM widget, "30+" by 4:24 PM), with ~10 archived — the org churns staff.
4. **The product surfaces shown for Grok Bot itself:** bot onboarding categories, a community bot Marketplace, email handling, computer use, GitHub/Notion/Figma/Slack integrations, Cursor Cloud Agents management, memory & routines, shareable templates, and agent unit economics ("browser use is expensive").
5. **The working rhythm:** humans steer in chat; bots produce artifacts (RFCs, dashboards, menus, designs, work orders); work moves through queues with PRs and deploys; humans approve taste calls ("which one do you like?").
